How We Work

We’re a small, owner-run valet service. The two people you book are the two people who park your guests’ cars.

  • We arrive an hour early to set up signs, lighting, and a key box.
  • We keep valet events under 35 cars so guests aren’t left waiting.
  • We carry general liability and garagekeepers insurance and can send proof to you or your venue.
